Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Write to us.

How long should my video be?

A short clip is enough. Face the camera, use good light, and talk naturally for a while so Rooman Agent can capture how you look and sound. You can record in the browser or upload a file.

How natural does the avatar look?

It is built from your own footage and lip-synced to each new script. Every generated frame is compared with your original face, and frames that drift are flagged or rejected, so your avatar keeps looking like you.

Which languages can my agent speak?

You choose the language when you describe your agent. Ask us which languages are available for your account today.

What happens to my data, and who can use my face?

Your avatar is only built after you give consent, and it is tied to your account. Write to us for the full details on how your video and voice are stored.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on how many minutes of video and live conversation you use. Get in touch and we will send you current plans.
